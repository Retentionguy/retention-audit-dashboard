Questions

Common questions

Will it sound like a robot?

No. Messages are short, written in your tone and spaced out like an attentive team would send them. Calls are always made by a real person.

What if a lead replies while follow-ups are still scheduled?

Everything stops instantly and your team is told to reply. Nobody gets “we're trying to reach you” texts while they're already talking to you.

What happens with enquiries that come in after hours?

They get an instant email and WhatsApp reply with your booking link, so they can choose a time themselves. Your team calls them as soon as you open.

Do I need new software or a new phone system?

No. We set everything up and connect it to the forms and ads you already use. You get a simple dashboard and a task list.

Can I change the messages later?

Yes. Any message, timing or channel can be changed whenever you like.

Who answers the calls?

Your own team, or a person you nominate. We'll agree together who picks up during opening hours, because that's what makes the speed count.
